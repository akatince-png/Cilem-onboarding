## Which data is processed and why

**Visiting the website.** When you open this website, the hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of access and the browser used. This is necessary to deliver the website securely (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR). Log data is deleted by the hosting provider after a short period.

**Enquiries.** If you contact us via the form or by email, the details you provide (name, email address, organisation, details of the assignment) are processed in order to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, to prepare and perform a contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR). Enquiries that do not lead to an assignment are deleted after TODO months; data relating to assignments is kept for as long as required by tax and accounting law (generally six years).

**Analytics (only with consent).** With your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations), this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data such as the pages visited and approximate location. No analytics cookies are set before you consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, please use the contact details under Legal information.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), telephone 0303 123 1113.
