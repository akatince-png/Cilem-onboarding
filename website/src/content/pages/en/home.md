## Working together

Every assignment starts with a short briefing: the subject matter, the participants, the setting and any documents that help with preparation. This allows terminology to be researched in advance and the interpreting mode to be matched to the situation – whether a confidential client meeting, a court hearing or a multi-day conference.

All information shared before, during and after an assignment is treated as strictly confidential.
